# ELSTELLA & CO. — BRAND SYSTEM

## Primary identity
**Name:** ELSTELLA & CO.  
**Descriptor:** Biological Restoration & Therapeutics  
**Scientific foundation:** Systems-Level Biology

Use the full company name in formal documents. Use Elstella in ordinary prose and interface labels where space is limited.

## Brand hierarchy
1. ELSTELLA & CO. — company identity.
2. Biological Restoration & Therapeutics — company descriptor, not a claim that a therapy exists.
3. PROJECT GENESIS — initial flagship research program.
4. Elstella Research Atlas — research-software workspace.
5. PROJECT CARDIA — separate exploratory research program.

## Writing rules
- Describe Elstella as an early-stage research initiative until its legal and operational status is formally established.
- Describe programs as research programs, not products.
- Use “proposed,” “under investigation,” “prototype,” and “not yet validated” when accurate.
- Do not claim that Elstella has discovered, invented, patented, validated, or clinically tested a therapy unless documentary evidence supports that exact claim.
- Do not imply that the company is incorporated, funded, partnered, or institutionally affiliated unless that status is verified.

## Visual direction
- Primary palette: deep graphite, blue-black, muted teal, mineral white, and restrained warm-metal accents.
- Typography: clear contemporary sans serif for interfaces; restrained editorial typography for long-form research documents.
- Motifs: biological organization, developmental pattern, tissue architecture, systems maps, and precise technical drawing.
- Avoid stock DNA helix imagery as the default identity. The brand should communicate systems biology, not reduce the company to generic genetics imagery.

## Standard footer
“ELSTELLA & CO. · Biological Restoration & Therapeutics · Research-stage work. Evidence before claims.”
