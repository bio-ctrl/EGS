# ELSTELLA & CO. — BRAND SYSTEM

## Primary identity

**Name:** ELSTELLA & CO.  
**Descriptor:** Biological Restoration & Therapeutics  
**Scientific foundation:** Systems-Level Biology  
**Company model:** Research-led biotechnology supported by computational infrastructure

## Brand hierarchy

1. **ELSTELLA & CO.** — company identity.
2. **Biological Restoration & Therapeutics** — company descriptor, not a claim that a therapy exists.
3. **Elstella Intelligence** — initial computational research capability.
4. **Elstella Therapeutics** — long-term therapeutic-development business.
5. **Division 01: Cellular & Mitochondrial Health** — first research priority.
6. **Division 02: Muscle & Physical Health** — second planned division.
7. **Division 03: Brain & Cognitive Health** — third planned division.
8. **PROJECT GENESIS** — foundational systems-level biology research.
9. **PROJECT CARDIA** — separate exploratory cardiac research track.
10. **VYREON** — preliminary concept associated with CARDIA only.

## Writing rules

- Describe Elstella as an early-stage research initiative until legal and operational status is formally established.
- Describe Elstella Intelligence as an early software prototype until validated with users.
- Describe the three divisions as ordered strategic priorities, not active therapeutic programs.
- Use "proposed," "under investigation," "prototype," and "not yet validated" when accurate.
- Do not claim that Elstella has discovered, invented, patented, validated, or clinically tested a therapy unless documentary evidence supports that exact claim.
- Do not imply incorporation, funding, partnership, or institutional affiliation unless verified.
- Preserve the separate status of GENESIS, CARDIA, and VYREON.

## Visual direction

- Primary palette: deep graphite, blue-black, muted teal, mineral white, and restrained warm-metal accents.
- Typography: clear contemporary sans serif for interfaces; restrained editorial typography for long-form research documents.
- Motifs: cellular energy, tissue architecture, organ systems, biological organization, and precise technical drawing.
- Avoid generic DNA-helix or molecule imagery as the only identity.

## Standard footer

"ELSTELLA & CO. · Biological Restoration & Therapeutics · Research-stage work. Quality and evidence before scale."
