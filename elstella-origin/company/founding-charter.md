# ELSTELLA & CO. — FOUNDING CHARTER

**Company identity:** Biological Restoration & Therapeutics  
**Scientific foundation:** Systems-Level Biology  
**Stage:** Early research infrastructure and hypothesis development

## 1. Mission

To understand biological systems across scales and investigate, with scientific rigor, how biological function is established, maintained, disrupted, and potentially restored.

## 2. Vision

To build a research-driven biotechnology company that connects systems-level biological understanding with computational methods and, only when justified by evidence, responsibly developed therapeutic programs.

## 3. Scientific thesis

Biological outcomes emerge from interactions among genetic regulation, cells, tissues, developmental history, physiology, and environment. Elstella's working thesis is that studying these relationships in context may reveal useful research questions that are obscured by isolated measurements.

This is a thesis to test, not a demonstrated discovery, proprietary advantage, or proof that any intervention will work.

## 4. Initial research architecture

- **PROJECT GENESIS — flagship research program:** literature-led and computational study of developmental systems, gene regulation, and biological form.
- **Elstella Research Atlas — research infrastructure:** a provenance-first workspace for organizing sources, data metadata, hypotheses, and review decisions.
- **PROJECT CARDIA — separate research track:** an exploratory literature program concerning cardiac remodeling and fibrosis. It is not the flagship program and remains independently gated.
- **Future programs:** aging biology, regenerative biology, mitochondrial biology, and other domains may be considered only after a defined question, feasibility review, and resource decision.

## 5. Operating model

Elstella is intended to develop as a research-led biotechnology company. The company should earn the right to advance each program through evidence, not ambition or presentation.

1. Identify a specific biological or research-workflow problem.
2. Establish what is already known through a traceable literature review.
3. Determine whether suitable public data can answer a narrow question.
4. Build and test reproducible computational methods.
5. Obtain qualified independent review.
6. Consider supervised experimental collaboration only when the evidence and institutional context justify it.
7. Advance a therapeutic program only through appropriate scientific, safety, regulatory, and professional oversight.

## 6. Operating principles

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Reproducibility over presentation.
3. Negative and contradictory findings are first-class evidence.
4. Distinguish association, mechanism, causation, and intervention.
5. State model-system and dataset limitations.
6. Do not claim novelty without a documented prior-art review.
7. Do not claim efficacy, safety, or clinical utility without appropriate validation.
8. Invite independent scientific criticism.
9. Allocate resources to measurable milestones.
10. Stop or revise weak hypotheses when evidence requires it.

## 7. What Elstella is not claiming today

Elstella has not established a medicine, validated therapeutic target, new biological mechanism, reversal of aging, clinical benefit, regulatory approval, or commercial product. Current work is research planning, literature organization, and early software development.

## 8. Milestone logic

- **Gate 0 — Definition:** a precise question, scope, and falsification criteria.
- **Gate 1 — Evidence:** a documented review and source-quality assessment.
- **Gate 2 — Data:** a lawful, fit-for-purpose public dataset inventory.
- **Gate 3 — Reproducibility:** analysis that can be independently rerun and audited.
- **Gate 4 — Independent review:** qualified external critique and revision.
- **Gate 5 — Institutional pathway:** any experimental work is considered only through qualified institutions, supervision, and required approvals.

A calendar date, attractive interface, or investor interest does not satisfy a scientific gate.
