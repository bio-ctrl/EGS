# ELSTELLA & CO. — OPERATING MODEL

## Strategic intent

Elstella's intended model is a therapeutics-focused biotechnology company supported by computational biology and evidence infrastructure. The company is currently at an early research-planning and software-prototype stage; it is not yet a validated platform business or therapeutic developer.

## Two connected capabilities

### 1. Elstella Intelligence

A proposed research platform to organize scientific literature, public biological datasets, disease context, source provenance, and evidence uncertainty.

Potential users include biotechnology research teams, pharmaceutical research groups, and academic laboratories. The product hypothesis must be tested through user interviews and measurable workflow benchmarks. A working interface alone does not establish product-market fit or scientific value.

### 2. Elstella Therapeutics

The long-term proprietary therapeutic portfolio. The objective is to develop medicines for defined unmet medical needs, rather than operate only as a software vendor or research-services business.

Potential future commercial models include:
- Development and commercialization of approved proprietary medicines.
- Licensing of validated intellectual property or therapeutic programs.
- Research and development partnerships with biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies.

These are future business-model options, not current revenue or capabilities.

## Initial portfolio logic

- **Initial research priority:** mitochondrial and cellular energy medicine through PROJECT MITOS.
- **Adjacent future areas:** neuromuscular/muscle medicine and kidney/organ restoration.
- **Foundational research:** PROJECT GENESIS informs the broader systems-level biology framework.
- **Separate retained track:** PROJECT CARDIA remains independently gated.

Only one disease-specific question should receive initial priority. Do not treat the three portfolio areas as simultaneous drug-development programs.

## Resource discipline

- Start with literature review, public-data metadata, and safe computational research.
- Prioritize one narrow research question and one measurable deliverable.
- Compare the opportunity with existing treatments, active pipelines, and prior art.
- Seek qualified scientific feedback before making claims.
- Do not spend on prestige facilities, branding, or complex infrastructure before the core research question is defensible.
- Do not represent fundraising as revenue or personal wealth.
- Any future experimental work must occur through qualified institutional partners with appropriate oversight and approvals.

## Decision rule

A program advances only when it passes its written evidence gate. If the unmet need is not meaningful, the literature already answers the question, data are unsuitable, or no credible differentiation is visible, revise the question or stop the program.

## Governance

Formal company formation, contracts, equity, fundraising, securities matters, IP assignments, and institutional agreements require parent/guardian involvement and qualified professional guidance while the founder is a minor.
