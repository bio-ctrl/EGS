# ELSTELLA & CO. — OPERATING MODEL

## Strategic intent
Elstella's intended model is a research-led biotechnology company supported by computational research infrastructure. It is not yet a validated platform business or therapeutic developer.

## Two distinct value hypotheses

### Research infrastructure
The Elstella Research Atlas may become useful if it measurably improves the organization, traceability, review, and reuse of biological literature and public research data.

This must be tested against ordinary workflows. A functioning interface alone does not prove product-market fit or scientific value.

### Therapeutic research
A therapeutic program must establish its own biological rationale, evidence base, feasibility, safety pathway, and institutional support. Software output cannot substitute for biological validation.

## Resource discipline
- Prioritize one flagship research question at a time.
- Use lawful public literature and data where appropriate.
- Keep research infrastructure separate from claims about therapeutic efficacy.
- Do not spend on prestige facilities, branding, or complex infrastructure before the core research question is defensible.
- Do not represent fundraising as revenue or personal wealth.
- Formal company formation, contracts, equity, fundraising, IP assignments, and institutional agreements require parent/guardian involvement and qualified professional guidance while the founder is a minor.

## Decision rule
A program advances only when it passes its written evidence gate. If the evidence is weak, revise the question or stop the program.
