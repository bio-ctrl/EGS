# ELSTELLA & CO. — OPERATING MODEL

## Strategic intent

ELSTELLA is intended to become a therapeutics-focused biotechnology company supported by computational biology and evidence infrastructure. It is currently at an early research-planning and software-prototype stage.

## The early-company model

The company's operating philosophy is informed by the early history of Eli Lilly: begin with a clear medical purpose, establish quality as a defining standard, build scientific capability deliberately, collaborate with experts, reinvest in research, and expand only when evidence supports it. ELSTELLA is adapting those principles—not copying products, branding, or historical claims.

## Two connected capabilities

### 1. Elstella Intelligence — build first

A research workspace to organize scientific literature, public biological datasets, disease context, source provenance, and evidence uncertainty.

Potential users include biotechnology research teams, pharmaceutical research groups, and academic laboratories. This is a product hypothesis, not proven demand. Validate it through qualified user interviews and measurable workflow benchmarks.

### 2. Elstella Therapeutics — long-term

The intended proprietary therapeutic-development business. Potential future models include development of approved medicines, licensing of defensible IP, and research partnerships. These are future possibilities, not current capabilities or revenue.

## Three divisions, in order

1. **Cellular & Mitochondrial Health:** first research division. Initial work is a high-level landscape, unmet-need assessment, public-data inventory, and independent review.
2. **Muscle & Physical Health:** second planned division. Future medical focus includes muscle disease, weakness, and age-related loss of function.
3. **Brain & Cognitive Health:** third planned division. Future medical focus includes neurological disease, brain health, and cognitive decline.

Do not run these divisions simultaneously. Division 02 is activated only after Division 01 passes a documented review gate and resources permit. Division 03 follows the same rule after Division 02.

## Resource discipline

- Start with literature review, public-data metadata, and safe computational research.
- Build one useful software workflow and benchmark it.
- Seek qualified scientific feedback before making claims.
- Do not allocate capital to prestige facilities, branding, or complex infrastructure before the core research case is defensible.
- Do not represent fundraising as revenue or personal wealth.
- Any experimental or translational work must occur through qualified institutional partners with appropriate oversight and approvals.

## Decision rule

A program advances only when it passes its written evidence gate. If the unmet need is not meaningful, the literature already answers the question, data are unsuitable, or no credible differentiation exists, revise or stop.

## Governance

Formal company formation, contracts, equity, fundraising, securities matters, IP assignments, and institutional agreements require parent/guardian involvement and qualified professional guidance while the founder is a minor.
