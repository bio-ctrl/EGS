# SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY, CLAIMS & SAFETY STANDARD

## Evidence and claims

Every external scientific claim must be traceable to a source. Clearly distinguish:
1. a result directly reported by a study;
2. an interpretation made by the study authors;
3. an interpretation made by Elstella;
4. an untested hypothesis;
5. a demonstrated product capability.

Do not use a prototype screenshot as evidence of scientific performance.

## Prohibited overclaims

Do not claim:
- a medicine has been discovered or validated when it has not;
- a therapeutic reverses aging or disease without appropriate evidence;
- a platform is novel before prior-art and competitor review;
- a computational result proves biological efficacy;
- investor interest, funding or partnerships that have not occurred;
- regulatory approval or clinical readiness that does not exist.

## Research safety

The current program is literature review and safe computational work. Any future wet-lab research must be conducted through an appropriate institution, with qualified supervision, required approvals, training and safety controls.

No unsupervised toxin, venom, pathogen, hazardous chemical, genetic-engineering, animal or human experimentation is part of this project.

## Human data

Do not collect or upload identifiable patient information. Any future use of human-subject data requires institutional review, appropriate permissions, privacy protections and qualified oversight.

## Intellectual property

Maintain dated records of work and sources. Do not assume that a broad idea is patentable. Before making patentability or freedom-to-operate claims, obtain a professional prior-art and IP assessment. Do not publicly disclose potentially protectable details before receiving qualified IP advice.

## External communication

All decks, websites and proposals must state the actual stage of development. Use terms such as “proposed,” “under investigation,” “prototype” and “not yet validated” where accurate.

## Governance

Because the founder is a minor, formal company formation, contracts, equity, fundraising, securities compliance, IP assignments and institutional agreements require appropriate parent/guardian involvement and qualified professional guidance.
