Evidence standards

The platform must make it harder to overstate a result, not easier.

Provenance firstEvery extracted claim must link to a source and precise location.
Context preservedRecord disease, tissue, cell state, model, time point and endpoint.
Contradictions retainedDo not hide negative findings or force false consensus.
Independent reviewBenchmark against conventional workflows and seek domain-expert criticism.
Falsifiable valueIf the tool adds no measurable value, revise or stop the platform hypothesis.
