# ELSTELLA — RESEARCH PROGRAM STATUS

This document distinguishes the single active flagship research focus from retained historical work. Listing a concept does not mean that it is active, funded, validated, or supported by a therapeutic candidate.

## Active flagship

| Program | Strategic role | Current status | Immediate boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROJECT LEIGH | Sole flagship disease-area research assessment: Leigh syndrome spectrum | Initial charter and landscape assessment framework | Literature and public-data assessment only; no candidate, target, intervention, or therapeutic claim |
| Elstella Intelligence | Computational research capability supporting PROJECT LEIGH | Early software prototype | Must be benchmarked; not a validated discovery engine or commercial product |

## Retained historical and exploratory work

| Workstream | Status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| PROJECT GENESIS | Foundational research retained | Developmental systems, gene regulation, and biological form; not a second active therapeutic program |
| PROJECT CARDIA | Separate exploratory historical track | Cardiac remodeling/fibrosis work is not part of PROJECT LEIGH |
| VYREON | Preliminary concept associated with CARDIA | Not a molecule, drug candidate, target, or validated therapeutic |
| PROJECT MITOS | Superseded as the active flagship by PROJECT LEIGH | Earlier documents retained as historical research planning; not a parallel active program |
| Other prior concepts | Historical or parked unless explicitly reassessed | No implied active development or validated claims |

## Program rules

1. PROJECT LEIGH is the only active flagship disease-area research assessment.
2. Do not launch multiple disease programs in parallel.
3. Elstella Intelligence must demonstrate measurable value against an appropriate conventional workflow.
4. Keep GENESIS and CARDIA records separate; do not merge their hypotheses, evidence, or claims into PROJECT LEIGH.
5. Preserve prior research artifacts. Reclassify status transparently rather than silently deleting history.
6. No program may be described as a drug candidate, validated therapeutic, clinical program, or licensing-ready asset without the required evidence, rights, and oversight.
7. Advance, revise, or stop based on documented findings and qualified review.
8. CERES is explicitly out of scope and must not be accessed or modified.
