## Program rules

1. Division 01 is the only current research priority.
2. Divisions 02 and 03 remain visible in strategy and roadmap but are not active.
3. Do not launch multiple disease programs in parallel.
4. Elstella Intelligence must demonstrate measurable value against an appropriate conventional workflow.
5. Keep GENESIS and CARDIA evidence separate; do not merge their claims into Division 01.
6. No program may be described as a drug candidate, validated therapeutic, clinical program, or licensing-ready asset without the required evidence, rights, and oversight.
7. Advance, revise, or stop based on documented findings and qualified review.
8. CERES is explicitly out of scope and must not be accessed or modified.
