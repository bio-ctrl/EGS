# PROJECT CARDIA — CARDIAC FIBROSIS

**Program status:** Initial literature-research program  
**Therapeutic status:** No drug candidate; no validated intervention  
**Primary purpose:** Test the ORIGIN evidence framework on a clinically relevant, biologically heterogeneous problem.

## Research question

Can a context-aware computational framework identify meaningful differences in cardiac fibrosis across disease states, cellular populations, molecular mechanisms, model systems and stages of disease progression?

## Initial hypothesis

Cardiac fibrosis is not a single uniform biological state. Findings may differ across clinical contexts, cell populations, disease stages and experimental models. A structured evidence map may reveal these distinctions and expose gaps that broad disease-level summaries obscure.

This is a research hypothesis, not a new discovery.

## Scope boundaries

The initial review should distinguish at minimum:
- post-myocardial-infarction remodeling;
- pressure-overload and other heart-failure contexts;
- heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF);
- other relevant cardiomyopathy contexts when supported by the review protocol.

Do not pool these contexts without explaining why pooling is scientifically justified.

## Evidence dimensions

For each finding, record:
- disease and patient context;
- tissue and species;
- cell population and cell-state definition;
- model system and its limitations;
- study design and sample characteristics;
- molecular mechanism or pathway as reported by authors;
- intervention or exposure, if any;
- measured endpoint and time point;
- whether the result is observational, mechanistic, preclinical or clinical;
- contradictory findings and author-stated limitations.

## Candidate hypotheses to evaluate

**H1 — Context dependence:** Combining distinct cardiac disease contexts can obscure mechanisms that are specific to a particular context.

**H2 — Cell-state resolution:** Distinguishing fibroblast populations and states changes how candidate mechanisms are interpreted.

**H3 — Translation gap:** Some apparently promising antifibrotic signals rely on preclinical or surrogate endpoints and do not yet establish patient-important clinical benefit.

These are questions for structured review. Do not present them as confirmed results.

## Falsification and quality checks

- Compare the context-aware map with a conventional narrative or systematic-review summary.
- Have independent reviewers classify the same sample of papers.
- Measure disagreement and document ambiguous fields.
- Ask cardiovascular researchers whether the map changes their interpretation or prioritization.
- Record findings that contradict the initial hypothesis.
- If no measurable improvement is found, report that result and revise or stop the platform approach.
