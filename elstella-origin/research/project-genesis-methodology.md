# PROJECT GENESIS — COMPUTATIONAL RESEARCH METHOD

## Purpose
Define a reproducible, literature-led method for examining how gene regulation and developmental context are associated with differences in cell identity, tissue organization, and biological form.

This is a research-planning document. It does not specify genetic engineering, biological experimentation, or therapeutic intervention.

## Research question
Across selected, well-documented developmental and comparative biology studies, what relationships between regulatory context, developmental timing, cell identity, and tissue organization are consistently reported, and where are the evidence gaps?

## Work packages

### WP1 — Scope and terminology
- Define the biological concepts and terms used in the review.
- Separate gene sequence, gene regulation, gene expression, cell state, tissue organization, and organism-level phenotype.
- Record competing definitions rather than silently combining them.

### WP2 — Literature map
- Write a transparent search strategy before screening.
- Record databases, search dates, exact queries, filters, and inclusion/exclusion decisions.
- Distinguish reviews from primary research.
- Record model organism, developmental stage, tissue, cell population, measurement type, and study limitations.
- Treat search results as candidate sources until the original publication is checked.

### WP3 — Public dataset inventory
- Identify candidate datasets from established public repositories.
- Record accession, source repository, organism, tissue, developmental stage, assay type, sample count where documented, data-use conditions, and source URL.
- Do not download or analyze data until access terms and suitability have been checked.
- Do not place personal, patient-identifiable, or confidential data in this repository.

### WP4 — Evidence synthesis
- Summarize what each source directly reports.
- Separate author interpretation from Elstella interpretation.
- Label correlation, perturbation evidence, mechanistic evidence, and unresolved interpretation distinctly.
- Record negative, contradictory, and null findings.
- Avoid combining incomparable organisms, tissues, stages, or assays without an explicit rationale.

### WP5 — Feasibility and gap assessment
- Identify whether the evidence is sufficiently complete to support a narrow question.
- Identify confounding variables, missing metadata, and model-system limitations.
- Compare the proposed question with existing reviews and published work.
- State what evidence would falsify or materially weaken the working hypothesis.

## Minimum source record
Each source record should include a stable identifier, full citation, DOI or accession where available, source URL, publication type, organism, tissue, developmental context, assay, key reported result, limitation, reviewer, review status, and date checked.

## Analysis boundary
Initial work is limited to literature review, public-data metadata curation, and descriptive analysis of appropriately accessible data. No gene sequence design, gene editing, organism engineering, wet-lab experimentation, or therapeutic testing is part of this project.

## Completion criteria
The first phase is complete only when the search process is reproducible, source records are traceable, screening decisions are documented, major claims have source locations, limitations are explicit, and an independent reviewer can audit the synthesis.
