# PROJECT CARDIA — LITERATURE REVIEW PROTOCOL (DRAFT)

**Protocol status:** Draft for review; not registered and not yet executed.  
**Review type:** Scoping evidence map / structured literature review. Do not call this a completed systematic review.

## 1. Objective

Map how cardiac fibrosis findings vary across disease context, tissue, cell population or state, model system, study design and endpoint. Separately evaluate whether a structured context-aware evidence representation improves reproducibility and usefulness compared with an unstructured summary.

## 2. Review questions

1. Which cardiac disease contexts are represented in the selected literature?
2. Which fibroblast populations or cell states are described, and how are they defined?
3. Which model systems and endpoints support each reported finding?
4. Where do findings agree, conflict or remain uncertain?
5. What is the translational distance between preclinical findings and patient-important outcomes?

## 3. Eligibility — proposed

**Include:** peer-reviewed primary studies and relevant reviews addressing cardiac fibrosis, cardiac fibroblasts, fibroblast-state heterogeneity, immune–stromal interactions, cardiac remodeling or fibrosis-related outcomes in a defined cardiac disease context.

**Exclude:** non-cardiac fibrosis unless used explicitly as background; studies with no identifiable cardiac relevance; unsupported opinion pieces from the primary evidence synthesis; records whose bibliographic identity cannot be verified.

Do not impose language or date restrictions without documenting the reason. Record conference abstracts and preprints separately from peer-reviewed studies.

## 4. Sources and search log

Search at minimum PubMed and Europe PMC. Search reference lists of relevant reviews. Record the exact query, database, date, filters, number of records and export filename for every search. The initial search must be rerun and logged before reporting any counts.

Suggested PubMed concept search (refine and document before execution):

`("Myocardial Fibrosis"[Mesh] OR "cardiac fibrosis" OR "myocardial fibrosis" OR "cardiac remodeling") AND (fibroblast* OR myofibroblast* OR "stromal cell" OR "immune interaction")`

This is a starting query, not a validated exhaustive search strategy.

## 5. Screening

Deduplicate records. Screen titles and abstracts against the eligibility criteria, then assess full text where available. Record a reason for every full-text exclusion. A second independent reviewer should check screening decisions before the map is described as independently reviewed.

## 6. Extraction fields

Capture: verified title; authors; year; DOI/PMID; publication type; disease context; species and cohort; tissue; cell population/state; model system; study design; method; intervention/exposure if applicable; endpoint; time point; main result; exact source location; author-stated limitation; funding/conflicts; reviewer; extraction date; verification state.

Separate direct study results, author interpretations and Elstella interpretations. Do not infer causality from observational associations.

## 7. Synthesis

Use descriptive tables and narrative synthesis. Keep distinct disease contexts separate unless a scientifically justified grouping is defined. Do not generate an opaque evidence score. Report missingness, uncertainty, contradictory results and model limitations.

## 8. Reporting

Use PRISMA 2020 as a reporting reference where appropriate. PRISMA is a reporting guideline, not a substitute for a sound review method or a risk-of-bias tool. See https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020.

## 9. Amendments and transparency

Date every protocol change and explain why it was made. Do not retroactively present an exploratory search as preregistered. Preserve search logs, screening decisions, extraction forms and code versions.

## 10. Current status

No complete search, screening, extraction or synthesis is claimed by this protocol. The starter citations in the repository are unreviewed leads.
